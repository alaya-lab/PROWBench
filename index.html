PROWBench

Do Video Models Render What the Program Specifies?

Alaya Lab

Paper GitHub 🤗Dataset

TL;DR

PROWBench checks whether generated videos faithfully realize program-specified entity trajectories, camera motion, and interactions, using replayable world states and timestamped events as the reference.

Takeaways

  1. 01Proxy conditioning improves entity control over camera conditioning alone, with strong video priors also improving event alignment.
  2. 02Proxy videos already encode camera motion, allowing camera control comparable to several models given explicit camera poses.
  3. 03General-purpose video models are competitive with specialized renderers on short clips across control and event fidelity.
  4. 04Fine-tuning offers mixed gains, while reliable domain-specific events still benefit from dedicated training.
  5. 05Better proxies need to bind identity, appearance, orientation, and articulation as well as position and motion.
  6. 06Long-horizon entity reappearance and identity consistency across independently generated views remain unresolved.
  7. 07Interaction Success and Logic-Render Alignment reveal failures visual and spatial scores miss, but judge calibration remains a limitation.

Structural inputs

Coarse 3D, CWM proxy, and Colored-OBB are rendered from the same recorded world state and camera trajectory. The world record retains entity states and events even outside the camera’s field of view.
